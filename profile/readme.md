## What it does

Tower of Fantasy is an open-world anime MMO with action combat, climbing, and exploration. The offline mode lets you play without other players — but the grind remains.

This trainer removes the offline grind. **Infinite HP** keeps you alive, **unlimited stamina** lets you climb forever, and **skill no cooldown** turns combat into a flow state. **Unlock all weapons** gives you the full arsenal from the start.
